Integration of Theoretical Information Systems Models, Core Architectural Dimensions, and Agricultural Net Benefits

1. Theoretical Foundations

Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)

  • Perceived Usefulness (PU): Enhances yield prediction accuracy & transaction speed.
  • Perceived Ease of Use (PEOU): Intuitive React SPA with seamless Sui wallet prompts.

DeLone & McLean IS Success Model

  • Information Quality: Precise, tamper-proof telemetry data.
  • System Quality: High availability 3-tier modular architecture.
  • Service Quality: Role-tailored dashboards and dual authentication security.
2. TerraNode Architectural Dimensions

Information Quality & Data Integrity

  • SHA-256 payload digest calculation for sensor telemetry.
  • On-chain Move smart contract state binding (`ProduceBatch`).

System Quality & Modular Architecture

  • React 19 SPA frontend with Mysten dApp Kit integration.
  • Django 5.1 REST API backend with PostgreSQL storage.

Service Quality & Security Control

  • RoleGuard RBAC (Farmer, Logistics Handler, Administrator).
  • Dual Authentication: Simple JWT + PyNaCl Ed25519 signatures.

Predictive Analytics Engine

  • Weighted Moving Average ($WMA$) crop yield forecasting model.
  • Redis sub-second caching engine (10 min TTL).
3. Net Benefits & Outcomes

Tamper-Proof Audit Trail

  • Verifiable immutable history of farm telemetry and produce batches.

Smallholder Farmer Protection

  • Fair trade verification & elimination of fraudulent yield claims.

Climate-Adaptive Guidance

  • Data-driven irrigation, shading, and soil management recommendations.

Transparent Supply Chain

  • End-to-end verifiable custody transfer on Sui Layer-1 network.
